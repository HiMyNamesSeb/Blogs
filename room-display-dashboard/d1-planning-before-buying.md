# Day 1 - Planning Before Buying 🗺️

📅 **Date:** November 18, 2026
🧩 **Phase:** [Phase 0: Planning and buying](https://github.com/HiMyNamesSeb/room-display-dashboard/tree/main/phase-0-planning-and-buying)

Day 1 of my wall display. It will show my Google Calendar, Google Tasks and the weather over a slow pixel-art background, running on a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and a thin portable monitor. Nothing is built yet. Today was all about setting up the project and figuring out exactly which parts to buy and what they cost. 🛒

## ⭐ Favorite part
My favorite part was planning this all out. It feels good to make progress. ✅

## 🛠️ What I did

**Set up the project 📁**
- Made the GitHub repo with a parts checklist and a Phase 0 folder.
- Started this blog folder and added the project to my website.

**Picked the parts 🔌**

| Part | Item | Price | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🖥️ Monitor | WGK 15.6" W156F1 (1080p, matte, mini HDMI + 2x USB-C) | $44.99 | Amazon |
| 🍓 Computer | Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W | $16 (hopefully) | Local seller |
| 📺 Video cable | Mini HDMI to mini HDMI, 3 ft | ~$6-10 | Amazon |
| 💾 Storage | SanDisk Ultra 32GB microSD | ~$8-10 | Walmart |
| 🔋 Power | Anker 33W 2-port charger (USB-C + USB-A) | ~$25 | Walmart |
| 🔌 Pi power cable | Micro-USB, 1 ft | ~$3-6 | Amazon |
| 🧲 Mount | Heavy-duty Velcro | $0 (already have it) | - |

**Total: about $103-112 before tax.** 💸 That's over my $100 goal but under my $130 max. Only the monitor and Pi prices are real so far. The rest are rough estimates and may change.

The monitor comes with a USB-C cable, and both the Pi and the monitor use mini HDMI, which is why I went with a mini-to-mini cable. The charger's USB-C port powers the monitor and the USB-A port powers the Pi, so the whole thing runs off one wall plug.

**Things to check 🔍**
- When I pick up the Pi, make sure it actually says "Zero 2 W" and has the square RP3A0 chip. The older Zero W is too slow.
- Test the monitor with the Pi before the Amazon return window closes (January 31, 2027).
- Keep the dashboard light, since the Pi Zero 2 W only has 512MB of RAM.

Nothing bought yet. 🙅

## 💡 What I learned
Raspberry Pis are hard to find for cheap right now. 🍓 The big online stores were out of stock and resellers charge way more than the $15 list price, so the Pi turned out to be the hardest part to price, even though it's one of the smallest parts.

## 😤 Hardest part
It was frustrating trying to find affordable Raspberry Pis since they're all so expensive. I only found one listing, but it was a set, with the Pi listed as worth $16 within that set. So I'm hoping he makes an exception for me and sells me just the Pi. 🤞
