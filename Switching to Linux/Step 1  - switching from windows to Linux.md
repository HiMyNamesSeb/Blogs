# Step 1 — Switching from Windows to Linux

I recently decided to switch from Windows 11 to Linux for the following reasons:

- Spyware, bloatware, vulnerabilities, and paywalls
- Interest in cybersecurity
- Wanting to learn Linux for servers

Here are the steps I took to make the switch.

## Step 1 — Finding a Distro

The first step was finding a distro that fit my needs. My first thought was Kali or ParrotOS, but considering how new I am to Linux, I decided the best move was to pick something beginner-friendly where I could learn step by step. I went with **Fedora 44 KDE Plasma**.

## Step 2 — Installing and Switching

Going in completely blind, I just copied the ISO onto an SD card and tried booting from it — didn't work, lol. I didn't realize I needed a flashing tool. I first tried Rufus but it didn't work, so I went to Fedora's website and used their own official tool (Fedora Media Writer), then booted from it and finished the install from there.
