# What This Blog Is About


This is about learning the basics of Networking, as in understanding how we transfer data at a fundamental level 

The purpose of this was just for me write as I went along helping me understand it deeper, and for me to look back on in the future. And maybe someone who hasn't made the switch yet but wants to and can read this for inspo, idk

## Main parts:
	1. What is a network and how do devices communicate at a high level
	2. IP addresses — IPv4, IPv6, public vs private, subnets and CIDR notation
	3. MAC addresses and how they differ from IP addresses
	4. The OSI model — what it is, why it matters, and each layer explained  simply
	5. TCP vs UDP — how they work, when each is used, and why it matters for hacking
	6. Ports — what they are, well-known ports, how services bind to ports
	7. The TCP handshake — SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK and why attackers care about this
	8. DNS — how domain names resolve to IPs, why DNS matters in security
	9. HTTP vs HTTPS — how web traffic works, what TLS/SSL actually does
	10. SSH — how it works, key-based auth vs passwords, why it's secure
	11. ICMP and pinging — what ping actually does, TTL, how traceroute works
	12. Common protocols overview — FTP, SMB, Telnet, and why legacy protocols are dangerous
	13. Firewalls and basic packet filtering — how traffic is allowed or blocked
	14. NAT — how your router translates private IPs to a public one
	15. Putting it all together — how a real request like loading a website touches every concept above
